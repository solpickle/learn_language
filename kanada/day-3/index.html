ಅರ್ಧ ಕೆಜಿ ಟೊಮೇಟೊ ಕೊಡಿ (Ardha keji tomato kodi) means “Half a kilo of tomatoes, please.” Change ಅರ್ಧ (half) to ಒಂದು (one) for one kilo. To ask the price, say ಇದರ ಬೆಲೆ ಎಷ್ಟು? (Idara bele eshtu?) — “How much is this?”

Pattern: quantity + item + ಕೊಡಿ (please give). A sample shop answer is ಕೆಜಿಗೆ ನಲವತ್ತು ರೂಪಾಯಿ (Kejige nalavattu roopaayi, “40 rupees per kilo”); real prices vary.

Today's mission

Read the words →

Imagine you are choosing tomatoes. Say these aloud twice:

  1. Ask the price: ಇದರ ಬೆಲೆ ಎಷ್ಟು? (Idara bele eshtu?) · How much is this?
  2. Request half a kilo: ಅರ್ಧ ಕೆಜಿ ಟೊಮೇಟೊ ಕೊಡಿ. (Ardha keji tomato kodi.) · Half a kilo of tomatoes, please.

Then swap ಅರ್ಧ (half) for ಒಂದು (one). The sample price in this lesson is only for practice.
