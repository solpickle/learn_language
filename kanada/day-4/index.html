Build a location question

ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣBus nildanabus stop
ಎಲ್ಲಿelliwhere
ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ?ellide?where is it?

ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (Bus nildana ellide?) means “Where is the bus stop?” Put the place first, then ask ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (ellide?, where is it?). Swap ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ (bus stop) for ಅಂಗಡಿ (angaḍi, shop): ಅಂಗಡಿ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? · “Where is the shop?”

To understand a reply, listen for ಮುಂದೆ (munde, ahead), ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ (edakke, to the left), or ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ (balakke, to the right). ಇಲ್ಲಿ means “here”; ಅಲ್ಲಿ means “there.”

Today’s mission

Read the words →

Practice with a friend or imagine asking for directions:

  1. Ask ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (Bus nildana ellide?) · Where is the bus stop?
  2. Reply with one direction: ಮುಂದೆ ಹೋಗಿ (Munde hogi, go ahead), ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗಿ (Edakke tirugi, turn left), or ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗಿ (Balakke tirugi, turn right).
  3. To a driver, say ಇಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ (Illi nillisi) · Please stop here.

Swap “bus stop” for another place you know, such as the shop.

New Kannada wording, romanization and generated speech still need review by a fluent speaker. English is a learning aid, not a word-for-word translation.
