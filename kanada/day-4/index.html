Build a location question

Read these words →
ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣBus nildanabus stop
ಎಲ್ಲಿelliwhere
ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ?ellide?where is it?

ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (Bus nildana ellide?) means “Where is the bus stop?” Put the place first, then ask ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (ellide?, where is it?). Swap ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ (bus stop) for ಅಂಗಡಿ (angaḍi, shop): ಅಂಗಡಿ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? · “Where is the shop?”

To understand a reply, listen for ಮುಂದೆ (munde, ahead), ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ (edakke, to the left), or ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ (balakke, to the right). ಇಲ್ಲಿ means “here”; ಅಲ್ಲಿ means “there.”

Today’s mission

Read the words →

Use your phone map and act out a short Bengaluru trip:

  1. Show an address in Indiranagar and ask a local ಈ ಅಡ್ರೆಸ್ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (Ee address ellide?) · Where is this address?
  2. Tell an auto driver ಇಂದಿರಾನಗರಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋಗಬೇಕು. (Indiranagar-ge hogbeku.) · I need to go to Indiranagar. Show the exact map pin.
  3. Ask the bus conductor for a ticket to MG Road, then ask how long the trip from Marathahalli takes.
  4. Finish by telling your driver ಇಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ. (Illi nillisi.) · Please stop here.
