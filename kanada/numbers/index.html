Digits and words

For zero, ಸೊನ್ನೆ (sonne) is a common everyday word. ಶೂನ್ಯ (shoonya) is another form.

Tens + ones

From 21 to 99, combine the tens word with a one-digit number. The ending changes a little as the sounds join:

ಇಪ್ಪತ್ತು (ippattu, 20) + ಒಂದು (ondu, 1) → ಇಪ್ಪತ್ತೊಂದು (ippattondu, 21).

Here are examples with 1, plus 25. The same idea works with other ones too: 42 = 40 + 2.

Past 100

Say the hundred, then the remaining part. In these examples, ನೂರು (nooru, 100) becomes ನೂರ (noora) before the next number.

Romanized spellings are simple reading aids, not exact pronunciation notation. Listen to the saved audio. Number forms and generated pronunciation still need fluent-speaker review. Number reference: Kannaḍa Kalike ↗.
