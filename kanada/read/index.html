Notice the vowel marks

A Kannada consonant usually carries a short “a” sound. A mark changes that vowel. These are just the forms you can spot in the words above.

Letters can also join together. Words such as ಕನ್ನಡ and ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ contain joined forms. For now, learn their overall shape and sound; we can unpack joins later.
