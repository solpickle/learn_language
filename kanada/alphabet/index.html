Build a syllable

Choose a consonant

A Kannada consonant normally includes a short a: ಕ sounds like ka. Add a vowel sign to change that sound: ಕ + ಾ = ಕಾ (kaa).

ಅ (a) is already inside the consonant, so its form has no extra sign. The small mark ್ removes that built-in vowel: ಕ (ka) → ಕ್ (k). You can ignore that mark while starting out.
