“ನನಗೆ ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಕನ್ನಡ ಗೊತ್ತು” (Nanage swalpa Kannada gottu) means “I know a little Kannada.” Switch ಗೊತ್ತು (gottu) to ಗೊತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ (gottilla) to say “I don’t know.” Leave out ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ (swalpa) when you mean Kannada in general.

Try it: ನನಗೆ ಕನ್ನಡ ಗೊತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ · Nanage Kannada gottilla.

Today’s mission

Read Kannada · Day 1 →

Practise a short bus-stop introduction. Ask a friend to choose one of the people above.

  1. Say hello and ask their name.
  2. Ask where they live and whether they study or work.
  3. Ask where they are going; finish with “Nice to meet you.”

Repeat with a different pair, such as two girls, two boys, or an older person and a student. Hide the pronunciation and English for one last try.
