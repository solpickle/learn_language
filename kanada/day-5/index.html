ಅಂಗಡಿ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ?(Angadi ellide?) · Where is the shop?
ಅರ್ಧ ಕೆಜಿ ಟೊಮೇಟೊ ಕೊಡಿ.(Ardha keji tomato kodi.) · Half a kilo of tomatoes, please.
ಇದರ ಬೆಲೆ ಎಷ್ಟು?(Idara bele eshtu?) · How much is this?

Place + ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? asks where a place is. Quantity + item + ಕೊಡಿ makes a polite request. These are review examples from earlier lessons.
