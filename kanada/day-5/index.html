Sources and notes

Further reading
  • Kannada: A Self-Instructional Course, Kannada Abhivrudhi Pradhikara, Lesson 6, pp. 41–51: market dialogue and reusable quantity, price, and food-order patterns.
  • Let’s Get Bengaluru, Department of Languages—Kannada, p. 53: a beginner conversation pattern for names, work, and study.
  • KannadaMaadi, “Essential Kannada phrases for your first week in Bengaluru”: informal examples for autos, markets, and cafés.
  • Income Tax Department, Link Aadhaar: the official e-Filing service for linking PAN and Aadhaar.

Some Day 5 lines use familiar English loanwords in Kannada script for Bengaluru situations. These are learning prompts; Kannada phrasing, romanization, and generated speech still need fluent-speaker review. PAN–Aadhaar linking is shown at a general help desk because the official service is on the Income Tax e-Filing portal, not at a municipal office.
